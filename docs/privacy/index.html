What is this?

A curated collection of 800+ free games that are updated daily: puzzle games, word games, trivia games, logic games, geography games and more! All games are completely free and do not require an account or subscription.

What is a dle?

A dle, or daily game, is generally a game that changes everyday which has the same version for everyone that plays, often taking inspiration from Wordle.

Can I help?

Sure! You can suggest a dle, report a bug, or give general feedback. If you want to support monetarily, you can donate on Ko-fi. Lastly, you can look at the code on GitHub and propose changes, as this project is open source.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data The Dles collects and how it is used. We do not require accounts and do not ask for personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your favorites and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear your browser data. You can optionally export your favorites to transfer them between devices, this is done via copy/paste or download/upload of a simple text file.

Analytics

We use two analytics tools:

  • Umami (self-hosted): collects anonymous page views, referrers, browser, device, and country. It uses no cookies.
  • Google Analytics: collects similar usage data and uses cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”)

Under CCPA, Californian residents have the right to declare their preferences on the sale of data for advertising and marketing purposes. If you wish to change your preferences, click this link to launch our preference portal:

We use a third-party to provide monetisation technologies for our site. You can review their privacy and cookie policy at https://publift.com/privacy-policy/.

Third-Party Sites and Services

As a daily web game directory, we link you to other sites that have their own privacy policies.

We use Tally for our forms, for things such as suggesting dles or reporting bugs. You can read their privacy policy.

Our community is on Discord. Information you submit there is handled under their policies.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, delete, or object to the processing of your data. Since we don't store personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to the third parties above. You can also contact us below.

Children's Privacy

The Dles is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns, you can either send an email to mail@aukspot.com or message @aukspot on Discord.
